How it works

Three steps, one password handover.

  1. Admin registers the driver Details and licence are captured here, and a login is created straight away.
  2. Company changes the password They open the driver on their Drivers page and set a real password. They cannot create, rename or remove the login.
  3. Driver signs in The driver uses their own profile page, and can change their password again at any time.
