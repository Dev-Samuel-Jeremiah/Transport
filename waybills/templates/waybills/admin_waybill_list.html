Import Transit Tracking Sheet

Upload a prepared transit sheet to create and organise waybills automatically — the company for each row is matched from its Transporter Name.

{% csrf_token %}

How it works: Upload a transit tracking sheet, such as the WFP template, with one row per truck. Supported columns include STO, Transporter Name, Origin, Vehicle Id, Waybill No, Driver Name, Destination, MT, Commodity, and Transit Status. Each row's company is matched automatically from its Transporter Name against your registered companies — rows for different companies in the same file are sorted into the right company automatically, and a row whose transporter doesn't match a registered company is skipped and reported. You do not need to create an LTI first: rows are grouped by STO, or by LTI No. when present, and matching LTIs are found or created automatically per company. Accepted formats: .csv and .xlsx.
